Many small changes in one mod.
Required on the client and the server.

Report bugs and share ideas in the comments or on Discord.

My projects

  • Nekoro Pokemon: The Cobblemon modpack all my projects are made for.
  • Nekoro Gear: Crystals, metals, gear and building blocks.
  • Nekoro Coins: Coins for quests, shops and loot boxes.
  • Nekoro Shop: A server shop and a player market that use coins.
  • Nekoro Collector Edition: Copies of the building blocks you collect, plus bags.
  • Nekoro Random Drop: Extra drops from blocks, mobs and Pokémon.
  • Nekoro World Setup: Rules for the world, mobs and recipes.
  • Nekoro Quality of Life: Small changes that can each be turned off.
  • Nekoro FTB Chunks Cobblemon Icons: Pokémon icons on the FTB Chunks map.
  • Nekoro Dark Mode: A dark interface in several colors.
  • Nekoro Enchanted Books: A different look for every enchanted book.
  • Nekoro Font: A new font.

Features

  • Switch single enchantments off and on.
  • Sort and transfer buttons for containers.
  • Mouse shortcuts for moving items.
  • A short F3 screen.
  • Search in the Key Binds screen.
  • Cleaner tooltips.
  • Hidden popups and warning screens.
  • Every recipe unlocked, and no recipe book.
  • XP orbs are picked up at once and merge into one.
  • Auto walk and direction lock.
  • Hand refill, hotbar swap, borderless fullscreen and full brightness.
  • Double doors, faster ladders and no drift in creative flight.
  • Every feature can be turned off.
  • No key is bound by default.

Config

  • config/Nekoro/QualityOfLife/common.json: changes to the game.
  • config/Nekoro/QualityOfLife/client.json: changes only for your own game.
  • Keys are in Controls, under Nekoro Quality of Life.
