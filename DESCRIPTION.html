Many small quality of life changes in one mod.
Every feature can be turned off in the config, and no key is bound by default.
Made for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack, but it works with any mods.

Required on the client and the server.

Work in progress

All my projects are being prepared for a big update of my modpack. I'm changing them step by step so they fit together, and they should be ready by the end of this year.
Many graphics are temporary until the mechanics of all my mods are finished.

Features

Enchantment switching

  • Switch single enchantments off and on. A switched-off enchantment does nothing and is shown greyed out.
  • The anvil then lets you add an enchantment that would clash with it, so Fortune and Silk Touch can be on one tool.
  • Use the menu for the item in your hand, or quick keys for Silk Touch, Frost Walker, Knockback, Punch, Fire Aspect, Flame, Sweeping Edge, Riptide, Soul Speed and Thorns. Quick keys also work on the armor you wear.
  • Curses can't be switched off.

Sort buttons

  • Buttons above containers and your inventory sort by category, name or mod.
  • Sorting by category puts items from any mod next to the same kind of vanilla items: all pickaxes together with the best first, then axes, armor, food, materials, blocks...
  • Storage that scrolls is sorted as a whole.
  • The hotbar is never touched.
  • The buttons can be hidden for chosen mods in the config.

Transfer buttons

  • Move matching items or everything between a container and your inventory.
  • The hotbar is never touched.

Mouse shortcuts

  • Mouse wheel over an item: move it one at a time.
  • Shift + drag: move every stack you pass over.
  • Ctrl + click: move every stack of that item.
  • Space + click: move everything.
  • They also work on the inventory tab of the creative inventory.

Short F3 screen

  • Shows FPS, coordinates, direction, biome, day and time, and light level.
  • A key switches to the full F3 screen, coloured so the useful lines stand out.

Key Binds screen

  • A search box, and a search by pressing the key itself that shows only what is really bound to it.
  • Filters for bound, unbound, conflicting and changed keys.
  • A category switch.

Cleaner tooltips

  • Enchantments under their own heading, coloured for curses, normal and max level.
  • Durability on every item that can break.
  • The real colour of dyed items.

Popups

  • Hide tutorial, advancement and recipe popups, or any other popup by its class.
  • Their text can be shown in chat instead.
  • Off by default.

Recipes

  • Unlock every recipe for every player and hide the recipe book, which also makes joining a world with many mods faster.
  • Off by default.

XP orbs

  • Orbs are picked up all at once, and Mending works as usual.
  • Orbs lying close together join into one, which means fewer entities and less lag.

Auto walk and direction lock

  • A key makes you walk without holding anything.
  • Another key locks your looking direction to the nearest of 8 directions, so you walk in a straight line.

Other features

  • Hand refill: When the item in your hand is used up (a tool breaks, the last block is placed, the last food is eaten, the last Poké Ball is thrown), the same item from your inventory takes its place.
  • Hotbar swap: A key swaps the hotbar with the row above it.
  • Borderless fullscreen: Fullscreen runs as a borderless window, so alt-tab and a second monitor work without the game minimizing.
  • Full brightness: The Brightness slider goes up to full brightness everywhere.
  • Creative flight: No drifting after letting go of the keys.
  • Double doors: Opening one door of a double door opens both. Sneak to open only one.
  • Faster ladders and vines: Climb down faster while looking down.

More information

Config

  • config/Nekoro/QualityOfLife/common.json changes the game itself. On a server, the server's file counts.
  • config/Nekoro/QualityOfLife/client.json is just for your own game.
  • Every option is explained in the file.
  • Keys are set in Controls, under Nekoro Quality of Life.
