Many small quality of life changes in one mod: switching single enchantments on and off, inventory sorting and transfer buttons, mouse shortcuts in inventories, refilling the item in your hand, a short F3 screen, a searchable Key Binds screen, borderless fullscreen, cleaner tooltips and more.
Made mainly for my Nekoro Pokemon modpack, but it works with any mods.

Why this mod

This mod replaces many mods I used in my modpack. They are linked below, next to the feature that replaces them. Most of them probably do their job better than my version, so try them first.
I made my own so my modpack always has up-to-date mods with exactly what I need: things I was missing in the originals, and without the parts I don’t use.
Every feature can be turned off in the config if you don’t want it or already use another mod for it. None of the keys has a default key, so nothing happens by accident: pick the ones you want in Controls, under Nekoro Quality of Life.

Enchantments

• Switch single enchantments off and on again. A switched-off enchantment does nothing and is shown greyed out, and the anvil then lets you add one that would clash with it, so Fortune and Silk Touch can be on one tool. Curses can’t be switched off
• A menu for the item in your hand, and quick keys for Silk Touch, Frost Walker, Knockback, Punch, Fire Aspect, Flame, Sweeping Edge, Riptide, Soul Speed and Thorns that also work on the armor you wear
Replaces Toggle Enchantments

Inventory

• Sort buttons above containers and your inventory: by category (all pickaxes together with the best first, then axes, armor, food, materials, blocks...), by name or by mod. Items from any mod land next to the same kind of vanilla items
• Buttons to move matching items or everything between a container and your inventory
• The hotbar is never sorted or emptied by these buttons
• A key to swap the hotbar with the row above it
Replaces the inventory sorting, easy transferring and hotbar changing of Quark
• Mouse wheel over an item moves it one at a time, and Shift + dragging moves every stack you pass over
Replaces Mouse Tweaks
• Ctrl + click moves every stack of that item, Space + click moves everything
Replaces Inventory Essentials
• When the item in your hand is used up (a tool breaks, the last block is placed, the last food eaten, the last Poké Ball thrown), the same item from your inventory takes its place

Screen and controls

• A short F3 screen with FPS, coordinates, direction, biome, day and time, and light level. A key switches to the full F3 screen, coloured so the useful lines stand out
Replaces BetterF3
• Key Binds screen with a search box, a search by pressing the key itself that shows only what is really bound to it, filters for bound, unbound, conflicting and changed keys, and a category switch
Replaces Controlling
• Fullscreen as a borderless window, so alt-tab and a second monitor work without the game minimizing
Replaces Borderless Window
• The Brightness slider goes up to full brightness everywhere
Replaces Better Brightness Slider Respawn
• Tooltips: enchantments under their own heading, coloured for curses, normal and max level; durability on every item that can break; the real colour of dyed items
Replaces Clean Tooltips and the part of EnhancedTooltips I used
• Hide tutorial, advancement and recipe popups, or any other popup by its class, with an option to show their text in chat instead
Replaces Toast Control
• Unlock every recipe for every player and hide the recipe book, which also makes joining a world with many mods faster
Replaces Not Enough Recipe Book

World and movement

• XP orbs are picked up all at once (Mending works as usual), and orbs lying close together join into one, which means fewer entities and less lag
Replaces Clumps
• No drifting in creative flight after letting go of the keys
Replaces NoCreativeDrift
• Opening one door of a double door opens both (sneak to open only one)
• Faster climbing down ladders and vines while looking down
• Auto walk key, and a key that locks your looking direction to the nearest of 8 directions so you walk in a straight line

Config

config/Nekoro/QualityOfLife/common.json changes the game itself (on a server, the server’s file counts): enchantment switching, recipes, doors, XP, hand refill, the inventory buttons and the sort order
config/Nekoro/QualityOfLife/client.json is just for your own game: popups, F3, brightness, fullscreen, Key Binds screen, mouse shortcuts, tooltips and movement
Every option is explained in the file.

Requires

Minecraft 1.21.1: NeoForge or Fabric
Fabric also needs Fabric API
Install it on both sides: on a server it is needed on the server and in every player’s game. In singleplayer just in your game.
